# Social share image rules

The checked-in cards in `public/og/` are generated by
`npm run social-images`. The generator is the layout test: it measures every
text run with the same bundled DejaVu Sans font used by the renderer, then
fails before writing files when text leaves its parent, leaves the canvas, has
less than 4.5:1 contrast, or produces a PNG of 300,000 bytes or more.

## Rules and sources

- **Metadata:** use absolute `og:image` and `twitter:image` URLs. Declare the
  Open Graph image's type, width, height and descriptive alt text; the Open
  Graph protocol defines those structured properties and says `og:image:alt`
  should accompany an image.
  Source: [The Open Graph protocol](https://ogp.me/).
- **X card constraints:** keep `twitter:card=summary_large_image`; its image
  guidance calls for a roughly 2:1 image, at least 300×157, no more than
  4096×4096, and under 5 MB. PNG is supported. Our stricter generator ceiling
  is 300,000 bytes.
  Source: [X/Twitter Summary Card with Large Image documentation](https://developer.twitter.com/en/docs/twitter-for-websites/cards/overview/summary-card-with-large-image).
  Buffer independently documents X's 5 MB static-image limit:
  [Buffer image sizes and formats](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/ideal-image-sizes-and-formats-for-your-buffer-posts-JxHNGZFvf9).
- **Canvas:** generate a 1200×630 PNG. Vercel documents 1200×630 as its
  recommended OG image size, and Ahrefs recommends the same dimensions and
  1.91:1 ratio for clarity across devices.
  Sources: [Vercel OG image generation](https://vercel.com/docs/og-image-generation)
  and [Ahrefs' Open Graph guide](https://ahrefs.com/blog/open-graph-meta-tags/).
- **Crop safety:** keep every text element in the centered 630×600 region
  (`x=285…915`, `y=15…615`). This preserves the key content through the
  common centered square crop and the 2:1 crop (which can remove 15 pixels
  from the top and bottom). Buffer quotes LinkedIn's direction to limit rich
  media text to a centered title-safe area and recommends square artwork when
  sharing across networks. Apple's Messages guidance warns that preview
  images appear at varying sizes and advises avoiding image text; the short,
  centered headline is the minimum text needed to distinguish city cards.
  A cross-client implementation guide illustrates the centered square and
  2:1 safe regions.
  Sources: [Buffer](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/ideal-image-sizes-and-formats-for-your-buffer-posts-JxHNGZFvf9),
  [Apple TN3156](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3156-create-rich-previews-for-messages),
  and [CurlShot's social preview image guide](https://curlshot.com/blog/social-preview-image-sizes).
- **Headline and brand:** use one short, large headline. City cards lead with
  the city name; the home card uses only “Family adventures, mapped.”
  `frolicmap` stays lowercase and visible in the safe region. Apple recommends
  a short phrase when text is necessary, while Ahrefs recommends custom,
  branded share images.
  Sources: [Apple's asset best practices](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/asset-best-practices/)
  and [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/open-graph-meta-tags/).
- **Contrast:** all text must meet at least the WCAG 2.2 AA 4.5:1 minimum for
  normal text against the solid panel behind it. The generator calculates and
  enforces this ratio.
  Source: [W3C Understanding SC 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html).
- **No fake controls:** do not draw button-like calls to action. Meta warns
  against deceptive interface elements in preview imagery; removing the fake
  button also leaves fewer words to survive small previews.
  Source: [Meta guidance on deceptive actions](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/177538719789272).
